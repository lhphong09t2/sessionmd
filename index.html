Technical FAQ

Permissions, Crash Snapshots & NotebookLM Details

Straightforward answers for security-conscious engineers, analysts, and knowledge workers.

Why doesn't SessionMD request <all_urls>, activeTab, or scripting permissions? +
SessionMD manages tab metadata, never webpage contents. Manifest V3 permissions are scoped strictly to storage, tabs, tabGroups, and alarms, with "host_permissions": ["https://api.polar.sh/*"] used solely when an optional Pro license key is activated or revalidated (zero <all_urls> or website host access). SessionMD cannot inject scripts, cannot read banking or internal admin DOMs, and never transmits your browsing sessions anywhere.
How does empty-window clobber protection prevent lost sessions after a browser crash? +
Traditional session savers often overwrite your saved state with a single blank tab when Chrome restarts after an OS reboot or crash. SessionMD maintains a 12-slot rolling ring buffer (sessionmd_crash_snapshots_v1) and automatically skips single-blank-tab auto-backups when your prior snapshot held real multi-tab work.
Which tracking parameters are stripped during URL normalization and deduplication? +
SessionMD removes utm_*, fbclid, gclid, dclid, msclkid, mc_cid, mc_eid, igshid, _hsenc, _hsmi, and ref_src, drops default :80/:443 ports and trailing slashes, sorts retained query keys deterministically, and merges pinned status and Chrome Tab Group labels onto the surviving tab.
How does the Google NotebookLM Source Packager format multi-workspace research? +
NotebookLM enforces per-notebook source count limits, making raw 200-tab research dumps painful to import. SessionMD Pro deduplicates URLs across your selected workspaces, groups sources under ## Domain Cluster headings with workspace provenance tags, and automatically splits massive archives into clean 350,000-character Markdown parts.

Paste your SESS-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX key inside the Pro & Bundles tab in the SessionMD popup and click Activate. Free users trigger zero outbound network requests; Pro keys revalidate over HTTPS with api.polar.sh once every 12 hours and include a full 7-day offline grace window for flights and airgapped travel.
